# Opening Patterns

Choose by available evidence, not by novelty.

## Scene → recognition

Use when the brief contains a real moment in which the author noticed something about the world or themselves.

```text
ordinary action
→ one detail changes its meaning
→ the detail exposes the article's central tension
```

Good local reference: `content/zh/growth/posts/2026-07-31-second-brain-as-survival-strategy.md`. Its opening moves from a specific act of organizing another person's thoughts to an unexpected recognition about the author.

Avoid cinematic decoration that is absent from the source material.

## Anomaly → mechanism

Use when experience produced two facts that should not coexist under the old explanation.

```text
expected improvement
→ unexpected cost or result
→ question about the hidden mechanism
```

Good local reference: `content/zh/ai-agent/posts/prompt-loop-engineering-practice.md`. Its opening pairs visible productivity gains with rising fatigue, then grounds the tension in repeated actions.

State both sides concretely. Do not label something “反常识” without showing the violated expectation.

## Capability → trust question

Use when the visible capability is not the article's deepest problem.

```text
plausible scenario
→ capability appears solved
→ one consequential question remains
```

Good local reference: `content/zh/ai-agent/posts/trusting-unattended-ai-agent.md`. Its opening makes the reader imagine an unattended workflow, then shifts attention to whether the output can be trusted.

The scenario must stay realistic and must not silently claim the author already achieved it.

## Scale question → earned answer

Use when the author has a concrete project result and the surprising part is how it was achieved.

```text
specific scale or difficulty
→ concise answer
→ evidence that makes the answer credible
```

Good local reference: `content/zh/ai-agent/posts/ai-native-blog-rebuild.md`. Its opening asks what team is required for a known body of work, then answers with the actual operating model.

Avoid trivia-style questions whose answer matters only because the author withholds it.

## Decision → cost

Use when the article concerns judgment under uncertainty.

```text
decision point
→ available paths
→ cost of choosing or delaying
→ principle the article will examine
```

Name the real loss, risk, time, money, commitment, or closed option. A decision without cost is merely a preference.

## Result → limit

Use for technical, research, and SEO-led articles.

```text
direct verified result
→ what it answers
→ what it still cannot answer
→ article scope
```

This mode can satisfy Answer-First while retaining forward pull. The open loop comes from the limit of the answer, not from fake suspense.

## Specific question → consequence

Use rarely.

A useful opening question contains a real object, constraint, and consequence. A weak one can be answered with “是”“否” or “看情况,” or merely repeats the title.

After the question, provide evidence or a provisional answer immediately. Never stack multiple questions.
