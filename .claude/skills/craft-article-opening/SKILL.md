# Craft Article Opening

Write an honest entrance, not a marketing hook. The opening must earn attention from the article's real material and point naturally toward its central claim.

Announce use of this skill before changing an article.

## Inputs

Read:

- the upstream brief or confirmed claim;
- approved author scenes, actions, evidence, and uncertainty;
- the current article body when revising;
- search intent only to understand reader context;
- `references/opening-patterns.md` to choose the entrance mode.

Never invent a scene, quotation, feeling, result, chronology, or level of certainty for a stronger hook.

## Design the entrance

Identify four elements:

```text
reader state → concrete anchor → unresolved tension → article direction
```

- **Reader state**: what the intended reader is doing, fearing, deciding, or misunderstanding.
- **Concrete anchor**: a person, action, object, number, failure, decision, or observed moment.
- **Unresolved tension**: the honest gap that makes the next sentence necessary.
- **Article direction**: an index line that points toward the central question without summarizing the whole article.

The index line acts as a hidden table of contents: it tells readers what kind of journey they have entered and which tension the article will resolve. It may sit at the end of paragraph one or in paragraph two.

## Choose one entrance mode

Prefer the strongest available real material:

1. **Scene** — begin at the moment something changed or became visible.
2. **Anomaly** — show a result that conflicts with the author's previous assumption.
3. **Decision and cost** — begin where the author had to choose and give something up.
4. **Concrete result** — lead with an observed outcome, then reveal the unresolved mechanism.
5. **Direct question** — use only when the question is specific enough that a reader cannot answer it automatically.

Do not force scene-writing onto tutorials or research notes. For explanatory content, lead with the surprising result or direct answer, then expose the question it leaves open.

## Draft and select

Privately draft three structurally different openings using real material. Do not make three cosmetic rewrites of the same sentence.

Score them with the three-second test:

- Do the first 35 Chinese characters contain something concrete or consequential?
- Does the first paragraph create forward motion?
- Is the tension relevant to the article's confirmed claim?
- Could only this author plausibly open this way?
- Will the body fully repay the attention promise?

Keep only the strongest opening in the article. Preserve alternatives only when the user asks to compare them.

## Shape the first screen

Use one short natural paragraph when possible; two or three short paragraphs are acceptable when rhythm requires a reveal.

Usually:

- paragraph one creates presence or tension;
- paragraph two clarifies why it matters;
- by paragraph three the reader can name the article's real question or answer.

For how-to and explanatory search content, place the direct answer within the first 40–100 words. For essays and first-person reflection, allow the scene to breathe, but do not delay the governing tension beyond the first screen.

Use the core search term naturally within the first 100 Chinese characters only when it belongs there. Never insert a keyword into dialogue, memory, or imagery merely for SEO.

## Reject weak openings

Rewrite if the opening uses:

- “在 AI 时代”“随着信息爆炸”“近年来” or other historical throat-clearing;
- “你是否……”“想象一下……” or a generic rhetorical question;
- an unsupported big number or absolute prediction;
- false suspense that the article cannot repay;
- a quote detached from a lived or argumentative context;
- a dictionary definition;
- a summary list before the reader has a reason to care;
- “不是 X，而是 Y” as a title-like thesis;
- polished abstraction with no person, action, object, evidence, or consequence;
- several competing hooks in the same paragraph.

Do not confuse loudness with attraction. Urgency, fear, identity pressure, and exaggerated novelty are not substitutes for relevance.

## Review the opening

Score 30 points:

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Immediate specificity | 5 |
| Honest tension | 5 |
| Relevance to the core claim | 5 |
| Authorial presence | 5 |
| Forward pull | 5 |
| Payoff integrity | 5 |

Require at least 26/30 and no hard fail.

Hard fails:

- invented author material;
- promise not fulfilled by the body;
- opening points to a different article than the confirmed brief;
- clickbait, privacy leakage, or factual distortion;
- first paragraph remains generic after removing topic keywords.

When reviewing an existing opening, output:

```text
Verdict: KEEP | REVISE | REPLACE
Score: N/30
Entry mode:
Concrete anchor:
Unresolved tension:
Index line:
Payoff location:
Required change:
```

After replacement, reread the ending. The opening and conclusion must resolve the same tension; otherwise fix the argument, not only the first paragraph.
