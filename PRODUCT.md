## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors to Xinwei Xiong's bilingual personal blog explore his public work, writing and travel photographs. The GET-290 brief asks for direct access to a work from the scene.

## Product Purpose

CUBXXW OS places the author's public products and writing in a navigable personal space. Product Lab provides a conventional, linked view of the same work.

## Operating Context

Hugo and PaperMod generate bilingual static pages. Native CSS and JavaScript provide the desktop, room, reading and product views. Three.js renders the Jiangnan room.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- `/projects/` and `/zh/projects/` retain the OS as the default; visitors can switch to Product Lab and back.
- Public product data comes from `data/products.yml`. Product status, source, access requirements and limits must stay visible and accurate.
- Notes and workspace state stay in browser storage. Product browsing does not authorize reading private notes or making external changes.
- Companion stays available on demand, without a permanent Dock item.
- Rendering failures retain the existing workspace recovery action.
- The homepage layout, article content and URLs are outside this task.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the existing Jiangnan scene and the author's real screenshots, writing and photography. The GET-290 brief names Ambi as a comparison reference, not as permission to copy its identity.

## Evidence on Hand

`data/products.yml`, localized product screenshots, `assets/js/space/`, `layouts/partials/space/`, and `DESIGN.md` establish the current product and implementation. GET-290 supplies the scope and acceptance criteria. No testimonials or new maturity claims are authorized.

## Open Decisions

No new standing image-generation workflow preference is recorded. This session uses the existing code and assets under the author's direct-execution authorization.
