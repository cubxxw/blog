# Jiangnan café resident — local implementation, 2026-10-21

Uses the same **Cat by J-Toastie**, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), from [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/DJ9rpAhrh3). Original asset, retrieval source, hash and licence are preserved in [CAT-ASSET.md](./CAT-ASSET.md). Keep its visible author/licence credit when integrating the resident.

The source GLB is unchanged. Jiangnan-only runtime adaptations in `jiangnan-cat.js` / `world-cat.js` shorten the original long lower legs and neck, reduce the ear tips, smooth coincident surface normals, mute the magenta inner ears/nose, and render an ivory coat with a fuller torso, a 128×128 deterministic fur bump texture, fine head-attached whiskers, smaller amber eyes and blinks. The posed model is normalized to a small domestic-cat scale. Its full turning footprint is reserved by a 0.34m navigation radius. These changes do not apply to the original café.

The supplied `Cat.001|IdleCat` clip remains the only supplied animation. Walking is an original distance-driven, four-beat rig gait with upper/lower leg and paw articulation, gentle arrival easing, head and tail motion, and breathing. It is **not** a motion-captured walk. The rig has no separate ear bones, fur strands, eye morphs or anatomical muscle simulation. The Jiangnan resident now has an authored C-shaped sleeping pose, closed eyes, a head resting near its front paws and a tail wrapping round the body, a 2.6-second waking transition, and a settling transition on returning to its nest. `cat-sleep-pose.mjs` handles the supplied rig's sibling torso controls in their common source coordinates, adds one runtime subdivision and a corrective torso morph, and blends back to the same walking skin. Resting grounding does not mistake tucked paws for a raised walking foot. Its coat is rendered ivory over the original albedo mask, with a fuller torso; the original GLB is unchanged. Walking takes a foot cycle per approximately 21cm travelled. A `rest` route phase still means a quiet standing pause. The source remains a stylized low-poly asset; these changes do not make it a photoreal animal.

`jiangnan-cat-state.mjs` imports the room's current bounds and collider constants. It owns eight connected safe floor stops, turn and speed limits, variable dwell, route finding for calls, finite pet response, cooldown and still interactions. The environment supplies all elapsed time. Hidden, blocked, quiet and reduced-motion views must pass inactive flags / reset the clock. No independent interval or animation loop is introduced. Disposal aborts the asset request and releases meshes, skeletons, textures, image bitmaps, materials, animation actions, shadow and whiskers, including a load resolving after disposal.
